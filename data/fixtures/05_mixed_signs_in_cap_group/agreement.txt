SECTION 1.01  DEFINED TERMS

"Consolidated EBITDA" means, for any Measurement Period, Consolidated Net
Income for that Measurement Period:

  plus, in each case to the extent deducted in arriving at Consolidated Net
  Income and without duplication:

    (a) Consolidated Interest Expense;
    (b) the provision for income taxes;
    (c) depreciation and amortisation expense; and
    (d) Restructuring Charges;

  and minus, to the extent included in arriving at Consolidated Net Income:

    (e) non-recurring gains arising on the disposal of fixed assets.

  The adjustments set out in clauses (a) to (e) above are the only adjustments
  permitted in the calculation of Consolidated EBITDA. No other adjustment,
  normalisation or pro forma item may be taken into account.

  provided that the aggregate amount added back pursuant to clause (d) in
  respect of any Measurement Period shall not exceed fifteen per cent. (15%)
  of the Capped Adjustment Base for that Measurement Period, and any excess
  over that amount shall be disregarded.

"Capped Adjustment Base" means, for any Measurement Period, Consolidated Net
Income for that Measurement Period plus the aggregate net amount of the
adjustments permitted under clauses (a), (b), (c) and (e) of the definition of
Consolidated EBITDA, determined before giving effect to any amount added back
under clause (d).

"Restructuring Charges" means charges, provisions and expenses (whether cash
or non-cash) incurred in connection with the closure, consolidation or
reorganisation of any facility, business line or workforce of the Group.

"Consolidated Net Income" means the consolidated net income of the Group for
the relevant Measurement Period, determined in accordance with the Accounting
Principles.
