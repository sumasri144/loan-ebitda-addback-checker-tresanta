SECTION 1.01  DEFINED TERMS

"Consolidated EBITDA" means, in respect of any Measurement Period,
Consolidated Net Income for that Measurement Period:

  plus, in each case to the extent deducted in arriving at Consolidated Net
  Income and without duplication:

    (a) Consolidated Interest Expense;
    (b) the provision for income taxes;
    (c) depreciation and amortisation expense;
    (d) share-based compensation expense, provided that only non-cash
        share-based compensation expense may be added back pursuant to this
        clause (d);
    (e) Restructuring Charges; and
    (f) Projected Cost Savings;

  and minus, in each case to the extent included in arriving at Consolidated
  Net Income:

    (g) non-recurring gains arising on the disposal of fixed assets.

  The adjustments set out in clauses (a) to (g) above are the only adjustments
  permitted in the calculation of Consolidated EBITDA. No other adjustment,
  normalisation or pro forma item may be taken into account.

  provided that the aggregate amount added back pursuant to clause (e) and
  clause (f) together in respect of any Measurement Period shall not exceed
  fifteen per cent. (15%) of the Capped Adjustment Base for that Measurement
  Period, and any excess over that amount shall be disregarded.

"Capped Adjustment Base" means, in respect of any Measurement Period,
Consolidated Net Income for that Measurement Period plus the aggregate net
amount of the adjustments permitted under clauses (a), (b), (c), (d) and (g)
of the definition of Consolidated EBITDA, determined before giving effect to
any amount added back under clause (e) or clause (f). For the avoidance of
doubt, the Capped Adjustment Base is calculated without reference to any
amount added back under clause (e) or clause (f), and the deduction required
by clause (g) reduces the Capped Adjustment Base.

"Restructuring Charges" means charges, provisions and expenses (whether cash
or non-cash) incurred in connection with the closure, consolidation or
reorganisation of any facility, business line or workforce of the Group.

"Projected Cost Savings" means cost savings and operating expense reductions
that are reasonably identifiable and factually supportable, projected by the
Borrower in good faith to result from actions taken or committed to be taken,
provided that such cost savings are reasonably expected to be realised within
twelve (12) months following the end of the relevant Measurement Period.

"Consolidated Net Income" means the consolidated net income of the Group for
the relevant Measurement Period, determined in accordance with the Accounting
Principles.

"Measurement Period" means each period of three consecutive months ending on
31 March, 30 June, 30 September or 31 December.

SECTION 6.01  FINANCIAL REPORTING

The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender, within forty-five (45) days after
the end of each Measurement Period, a reconciliation of Consolidated Net
Income to Consolidated EBITDA for that Measurement Period, prepared in the
form of Schedule 3 (Reconciliation Format) and signed by a director.

SCHEDULE 3  RECONCILIATION FORMAT

Each reconciliation delivered under Section 6.01 shall state, in the header of
the document, the currency, the units scale, and the sign convention applied.
Amounts shall be stated using the SIGNED_EXPLICIT convention, meaning that
every amount which increases Consolidated EBITDA is stated as a positive
number and every amount which reduces Consolidated EBITDA is stated as a
negative number, and that the stated presentation label of each line shall
agree with the sign of its amount. Parenthesised negatives, separate "Add" and
"Less" columns, and absolute values are not an acceptable presentation.
